🛒 Official Founder Profile

Gopagani Arun

Founder & CEO — NestBasket

Gopagani Arun is the founder and CEO of NestBasket, India's real-time all-in-one quick-commerce price comparison and cart intelligence platform. NestBasket empowers Indian consumers to compare live darkstore grocery rates across Blinkit, Zepto, Swiggy Instamart, BigBasket Now, and Flipkart Minutes in a single tap.
